# Consonants

## Custom Added

| IPA | Articulation                   | Example | How to make the sound                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --- | ------------------------------ | ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| /q/ | Voiceless uvular plosive       | Null    | Make a /k/ sound, but move the contact point much farther back, toward the uvula (the small hanging structure at the back of your mouth). Release the air without vocal cord vibration. |
| /ɢ/ | Voiced uvular plosive          | Null    | Make a /g/ sound, but move the contact point toward your uvula. Release the air while vibrating your vocal cords.                                                                       |
| /ɸ/ | Voiceless bilabial fricative   | Null    | Make an /f/ sound, but do not let your upper teeth touch your lower lip. Bring both lips close together and force air through the narrow gap.                                           |
| /β/ | Voiced bilabibal fractive      | Null    | Like the one above ^^^, but voiced                                                                                                                                                      |
| /χ/ | Voiceless uvular fricative     | Null    | Make a /h/ or /x/ sound, but constrict the airflow near your uvula. Force air through the narrow gap without vibrating your vocal cords.                                                |
| /ʁ/ | Voiced uvular fricative        | Null    | Make a /χ/ sound, but vibrate your vocal cords while forcing air through the uvular constriction.                                                                                       |
| /ʋ/ | Voiced labiodental approximant | Null    | Make a /v/ sound, but reduce the friction. Bring your upper teeth close to your lower lip without creating strong friction, while vibrating your vocal cords.                           |
