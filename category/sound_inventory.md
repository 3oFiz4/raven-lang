## 3. Syllable Rules & Permutations

### Canonical Syllable Rule: `(C)V`
- Every word/concept root is strictly open: an optional single consonant onset `C` followed by a single vowel `V`.
- Codas are eliminated for maximum smoothness, clarity, and conciseness.

### Exact Root Space
- **Onset options (C):** 33 (32 consonants + 1 empty onset)
- **Vowel options (V):** 14 vowels

$$\text{Total Monosyllabic Roots} = 33 \times 14 = \mathbf{462}$$

- **V-only roots:** $1 \times 14 = 14$
- **CV roots:** $32 \times 14 = 448$
- **Total:** $\mathbf{462}$ atomic concept building blocks.

*(Multi-syllable compounds follow strictly chained `(C)V` patterns: `(C)V.(C)V` yielding up to $462 \times 462 = 213,444$ combinations).*
